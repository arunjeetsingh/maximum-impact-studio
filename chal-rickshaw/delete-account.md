# Chal Rickshaw! — Delete Your Account & Data

You can request deletion of your Chal Rickshaw! account and the data associated
with it at any time. Deletion is handled by Maximum Impact Studio (the app's
developer) and is free.

## How to request deletion

Email **[hello@maximumimpact.studio](mailto:hello@maximumimpact.studio?subject=Delete%20my%20Chal%20Rickshaw%20account)**
with the subject "Delete my Chal Rickshaw account".

- **If you linked an email address in the game** (used for purchases), send the
  request **from that address** so we can verify the account is yours.
- **If you never linked an email**, your server-side wallet is identified only
  by a random ID and contains no personal information (just in-game values).
  Contact us anyway and we will work with you to locate and delete it — or
  simply uninstall the app; an unlinked wallet cannot be connected back to you.

We complete deletion requests within **30 days** and confirm by reply.

## What gets deleted

- Your account (linked email address and sign-in identity)
- Your in-game wallet: kamai (in-game ₹), items, round progress, entitlements
  (including Remove Ads)
- Your purchase records held by us

## What we may retain, and for how long

- **Purchase/refund records required for accounting, tax, and fraud
  prevention** — retained only as required by applicable law (typically up to
  8 years), then removed. These records cannot be used to restore the account.
- **Anonymous, aggregated analytics** (gameplay statistics tied to a random
  install identifier, never your identity) — retained per our analytics
  provider's policy; on request we will also delete events tied to your install
  identifier.
- Data held by **Apple** or **Google** (payment records, store account data) is
  controlled by them under their own policies — deletion requests for those go
  to Apple/Google directly.

## Partial deletion

If you want your data deleted but wish to keep the account (for example,
clearing progress only), say so in your email — we can reset the wallet without
removing the linked identity.
