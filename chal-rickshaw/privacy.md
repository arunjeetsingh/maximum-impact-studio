# Chal Rickshaw! — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-06-02_

Chal Rickshaw! is a single-player arcade game from Maximum Impact Studio. It is
built to respect your privacy: the game runs entirely on your device.

## TL;DR

- **No account.** There's nothing to sign up for or log in to.
- **No server / no backend.** The game has nowhere to send your data.
- **No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers.**
- **No personal data collected.** We don't ask for, access, or store your name,
  email, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.

## What the game stores

The only data Chal Rickshaw! keeps is your **game progress** — things like your
high score and settings. This is saved **locally on your device** by the game
and is never transmitted off the device to us or anyone else.

## Data shared with others

We do not share any data, because we do not collect any. The app contains no
advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Note that if you installed the app through Apple **TestFlight** or **Google
Play**, those platforms collect their own diagnostic and installation data under
**their** policies — see
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and
[Google Play's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). That data
goes to Apple/Google, not to us.

## Children's privacy

Chal Rickshaw! does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes to this policy

If our practices change (for example, if a future version adds optional
rewarded-video ads), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above,
and describe exactly what changes.
