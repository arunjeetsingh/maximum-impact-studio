About

Arunjeet Singh (Arun)

Founder, Maximum Impact Studio

Arun is the founder of Maximum Impact Studio. He has worked as a product manager and engineer at Meta, Amazon, Salesforce, and Microsoft. He loves technology and is passionate about making sure everyone can access the best of it — so it doesn't stay cloistered behind closed doors. These days he's focused on using AI agents to build innovative software and games, like the recently launched TokenCounter.
